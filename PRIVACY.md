# Privacy Policy

Effective date：2026-11-21

Open Splat Capture does not collect, transmit or share any personal data. The
app has no network access, no accounts, no analytics and no third-party
services.

**Data the app uses on the headset**

- Passthrough camera images
- Headset pose and, if enabled in Settings, controller poses
- Depth data from the headset
- The headset's room setup (walls, floor and furniture), used only to place the
  guide markers during capture

Camera images, poses and depth are saved only when you record. Recordings are
stored in the app's own folder on the headset. When you export a recording, it
is written as a ZIP file to the headset's `Download/OpenSplatCapture` folder. Nothing
leaves the headset unless you copy those files yourself.

**Deleting your data**

Delete a recording in the app's Recordings page, or uninstall the app to remove
all recordings. Exported ZIP files are ordinary files in the Download folder and
can be deleted with any file manager.

**Contact**

Questions about this policy can be raised at
